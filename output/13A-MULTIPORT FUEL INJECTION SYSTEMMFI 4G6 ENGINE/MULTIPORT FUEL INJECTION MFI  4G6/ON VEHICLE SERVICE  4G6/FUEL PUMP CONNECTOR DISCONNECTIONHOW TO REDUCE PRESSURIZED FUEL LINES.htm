FUEL PUMP CONNECTOR DISCONNECTION (HOW TO REDUCE FUEL LINE PRESSURE)

When removing the fuel pipe, hose, etc., since fuel pressure in the fuel pipe line is high, do the following operation so as to release fuel pressure in the line and prevent fuel from running out.
1.Remove the rear seat cushion.
(Refer to GROUP 52A - Seat.)
2.Remove the protector to disconnect the fuel pump connector.
3.Start the engine and let it run until it stops naturally, turn off the ignition switch.
4.Connect the fuel pump connector to install the protector.
5.Install the rear seat cushion.

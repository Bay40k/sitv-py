GENERATOR DRIVE BELT TENSION CHECK
Use the belt tension gauge to check belt tension at the shown point or check deflection by applying 98 N (22 lbs.) to the shown point.
Standard value:
Tension:
245 - 490 N (55.1 - 110.2 lbs.)
Deflection <Reference value>:
9.0 - 11.5 mm (.35 - .45 in.)

2.Loosen the lock bolt.

3.AIR CLEANER ELEMENT (Replace)
The air cleaner element will become dirty and loaded with dust during use, and the filtering effect will be substantially reduced.
Replace it with a new one.
(1)Unclamp the air cleaner cover.
(2)Take out the air cleaner element, install a new one.
(3)Be sure when clamping the air cleaner cover in place that the cover is completed closed.

TROUBLESHOOTING HINTS
The starter motor does not operate.
•Check fusible link No.4. <A/T>
•Check the starter (coil).
•Check for poor contact at the battery terminals and starter.
•Check transaxle range switch. <A/T>
•Check starter relay.
•Check clutch pedal position switch. <M/T>
•Check theft-alarm starter relay.
•Check key reminder switch.

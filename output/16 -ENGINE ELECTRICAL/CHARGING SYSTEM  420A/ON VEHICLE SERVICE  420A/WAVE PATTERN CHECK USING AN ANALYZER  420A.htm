NOTE
The voltage waveform of the generator B terminal can undulate as shown at left. This waveform is produced when the regulator operates according to fluctuations in the generator load (current), and is normal for the generator.
In addition, if the ripples are abnormally high (more than approximately 2 V when the engine is idling), the cause is probably an open circuit between the generator B terminal and the battery due to a blown fuse, and the generator itself is usually okay.

NOTE
1.The size of the waveform patterns differs largely, depending on the adjustment of the analyzer's variable knob.

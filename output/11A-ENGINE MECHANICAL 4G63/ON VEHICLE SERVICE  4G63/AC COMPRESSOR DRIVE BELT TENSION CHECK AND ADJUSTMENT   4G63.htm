A/C COMPRESSOR DRIVE BELT TENSION CHECK
Use the belt tension gauge to check belt tension at the shown point (a) or (b), or check deflection by applying 98 N (22 lbs.) to the shown point.
Standard value:
Tension: 255 - 333 N (57.3 - 75.0 lbs.)
Deflection: 6.5 - 7.5 mm (.26 - .30 in.)
A/C COMPRESSOR DRIVE BELT TENSION ADJUSTMENT
1.Loosen tension pulley fixing bolt A.
2.Adjust belt tension with adjusting bolt B.

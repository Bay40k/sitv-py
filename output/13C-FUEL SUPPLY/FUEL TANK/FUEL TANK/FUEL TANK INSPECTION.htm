INSPECTION
FUEL TANK FILLER TUBE ASSEMBLY CHECK
<FWD-2.0L Engine (Non-turbo) and 2.4L Engine>
(1)Connect a clean rubber hose to the port A of the fuel tank filler tube assembly.
(2)Blow air into the hose. Check that the air flows out of the port B.
(3)Use a screwdriver or similar tool to close the valve. Blow air into the hose in this condition. Check that the air does not flow out of the port B.
